Shal is a small town on a side road into [[Keya]], the capital city of Verra. Shal at one point was a medium sized city but the majority of the town now lays abandoned and crumbling. Wether the people left on their own or were forced out by some disaster is unknown. The only remaining buildings lie in the center of the city around a square. There are a couple houses, some administrative buildings, a small general store, and a tavern.

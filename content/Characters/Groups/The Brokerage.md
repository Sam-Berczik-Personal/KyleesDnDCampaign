The Brokerage (also known as The Guild) is a somewhat below-board trade organization operating out of [[Keya]]. They acquire and transport items for clients, and seem to mostly to hire mercenaries/subcontractors to do the foot work. They approached us in [[Shal]] and offered us a [[0. Summary up to first guild job#Travel to Keya|job]] to transport a package (implied to contain drugs) to the Guild Hall in Keya. Once we completed that job they offered us [[0. Summary up to first guild job#Abadon's Alley Job|another job]] to recover a package that had gotten lost in a bad part of town. The Brokerage seems to deal mostly in illegal items, meaning it is very protective of it's clients identities. They don't appreciate prodding, something we learned first hand.

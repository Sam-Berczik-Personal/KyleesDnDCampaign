Barbarian (Path of the totem warrior - Bear) Level 5, Buff Goblin?, True Neutral

---
## Summary:
Unlike any other creature I have encountered, has many features that resemble a goblin but their overall build is closer to that of an Orc, and a big one at that. Their intelligence is quite limited, they seem to be on a similar developmental level as a young toddler, verbal communication is limited although they often seem to have some capacity to understand things said to them. A force to be reckoned with in combat, I have yet to see them get truly angry and I am not sure I want to. I am happy to have them on our side.

## Important Notes:
- Trouble fitting through doors
- Affinity for small animals

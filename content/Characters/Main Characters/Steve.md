## Summary:
Has very limited memories of past before joining our group, had a life in the kings guard in the city. He was seemingly a member of the troop convoy that was transporting the [[Twank]]. The convoy was seemingly attacked and then Steve lost his memory. He woke up in a ditch confused and wandered into a nearby tavern that the rest of us happened to be staying at. His memories of his past are hazy at best and we continue to uncover more of what happened as we go, but he seems to have been associated with some pretty wildly corrupt royals. 

Steve has also mentioned hearing voices in his head. I suppose hallucinations shouldn't be too surprising after whatever assumed head trauma caused his amnesia, but something about it unsettles me.

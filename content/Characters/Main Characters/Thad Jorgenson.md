## Summary:
 Expert brewer and rat-thrower, comes from a family of master smiths that has a tendency to sell to shady characters, or at least doesn't much care if they do. Thad seems to have separated from his family over some disputes over their clients and Thad's disinterest in smiting. Thad is interested in making money and would like to spread some good along the way if possible.

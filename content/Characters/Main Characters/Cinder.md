Bard (College of lore), Level 6, Aasimar, Chaotic Neutral

## Summary
Adventurous and pragmatic, has proved her commitment to the group on several occasions, however she doesn't appear to be totally mortal. She is much more powerful than the rest of us and seemed to have triggered some sort of group dream hallucination early on in our travels together, although I'm not convinced that was her, I get the impression she has a darker side that I don't know the whole story of, keep an eye on her.

## Important Notes:
- Knows revivify
- Plays the flute
- Tendency to fireball

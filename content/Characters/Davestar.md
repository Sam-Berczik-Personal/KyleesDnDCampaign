## Summary:
Davestar was/is a member of a shadowy assassin organization, The Black Hand. He was brainwashed into joining their ranks but rose to become their most accomplished assassin. He eventually broke free of their control and escaped but they hunt him constantly. They are eager, maybe even desperate to have him back in their arsenal. He is clueless to most of the world, having spent the majority of his life a brainwashed killing machine.
